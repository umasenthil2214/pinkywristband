Privacy by design

An identifier, not a tracker.

The PINKY band holds one small Smart Chip and nothing else. It only answers when a staff phone is held right next to it.

  • No GPS, no live location. The band has no location hardware, and PINKY keeps no location history.
  • The chip ID is never stored as-is. It is hashed on the server before it is saved.
  • Each person sees only their own part. Parents see only their children. Staff see their daycare. Access rules are enforced by the database, not just the app.
  • Lost band? Unlink it. An admin removes the band from the child's profile and links a new one in seconds.

FAQ

Questions daycares ask.

Does the band track where my child is?

No. The band has no GPS and no battery, and PINKY keeps no location history. It only identifies the child when a staff phone is held right next to it, for example at check-in or check-out.

Does the band need charging?

Never. The Smart Chip is powered by the phone during the tap, so there is nothing to charge or replace.

Can children wear it during water play and handwashing?

Yes. The band is waterproof silicone with the chip sealed inside.

What do staff need to scan a band?

A phone with the PINKY Staff app. Most current iPhones and Android phones can read the PINKY Smart Chip. Staff can also check a child in manually if a band isn't on hand.

Do parents pay anything?

No. The PINKY Family app is free for parents and guardians. The daycare pays one simple monthly fee.

What happens if a band is lost?

An admin unlinks the lost band from the child's profile and links a new one. The old band stops working for that child right away.

Who can see my child's information?

Only the people who should. Parents see their own children. Staff see the children at their daycare. These rules are enforced by the database itself, not only by the apps.
